Nested `emit_expr` classifies raw Surface forms without revisiting the public reservation entrance, so a late lowering fix cannot protect an enclosing result/await/temp allocation. The repair belongs in the existing borrowed owning-input census.

The tag is semantically binding: `ThreadingStep::Bare(Keyword)` reserves that member text; `Bare(other)` walks as an ordinary expression even when it is a List. `Call` uses call-step roles. Thus a tagged Bare containing a genuine raw thread discovers that nested thread through ordinary recursion, while a keyword-headed data list in Bare does not gain a call role.
